#  **S – Situation**

A global tech salary dataset contained inconsistencies in country names, unclean categorical values, and unstandardized columns. The team needed a clean dataset and clear salary insights across experience levels, job types, locations, and time periods to understand compensation trends in the tech industry.

# **T – Task**

My objective was to:

Clean and standardize all country and category fields

Prepare the dataset for analysis

Build visualizations that reveal meaningful salary patterns across experience, geography, employment type, and company size

Extract actionable insights from these patterns

# **A – Action**
**1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**

Corrected inconsistent country names and mapped them to standardized formats

Cleaned category fields such as experience level, job type, and company size

Ensured data types (numeric, categorical, date) were properly formatted

Removed or corrected entries that could distort salary calculations

This produced a clean and consistent dataset ready for analytical modeling.

**2. Visualization & Insights Extraction**

I created targeted visualizations that revealed several strong, data-backed insights:

***Salary Insights***

Experience-Level Impact:
Mean salaries increased consistently from entry-level → mid-level → senior → executive.
Executive-level roles had the highest average salary across the dataset.

Employment Type Differences:
Full-time positions offered the highest salaries, while contract and freelance roles showed wider variability and lower central values.

Company Size Influence:
Large companies (L) offered the highest mean salaries, followed by medium (M), with small firms (S) paying significantly less on average.

Year-over-Year Salary Trends:
Average salaries showed a steady upward trend, indicating increasing global demand for tech talent.

***Geographic Insights***

Country-level visualizations revealed major differences:
Some countries consistently paid above the global median, while others lagged significantly, reflecting economic and market maturity differences.

***Workforce Composition Patterns***

Experience vs Job Category:
Certain job categories had a higher concentration of senior and mid-level professionals.

Company Size vs Job Roles:
Large companies employed a more diverse range of job types, while small companies showed specialization in specific roles.

# **R – Result**

The project resulted in:

A fully cleaned dataset with standardized geographic and categorical fields

Visualization-driven insights exposing how experience, employment type, company size, and country influence salaries

Clear patterns in global tech compensation and workforce distribution

A reproducible Python notebook workflow showcasing strong skills in data cleaning, feature mapping, visualization, and insight storytelling

This project demonstrates my ability to take raw data, clean it thoroughly, visualize it effectively, and turn it into decision-ready insights that help understand global salary structures.
